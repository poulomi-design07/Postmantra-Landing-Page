What services can Post Mantra manage?

We support social media marketing, content planning and creation, creator seeding, paid social campaigns, community management and strategy.

How do we get started?

Message us with your business, goals and channels. We will discuss your needs, agree on a scope and share the next steps.

Do you work with our existing team?

Yes. We can coordinate with your team and agree on who provides inputs, reviews content and approves publishing.

Is paid media spend included in the service fee?

Media spend is separate from service fees unless your written agreement says otherwise. We confirm the budget with you before a campaign begins.

How soon will we see results?

Timing depends on your goals, audience, offer, budget and consistency. We set expectations together and review progress regularly. We do not promise the same result for every business.

What do you need from us?

We usually need your goals, brand materials, product details, account access and timely feedback. The exact needs depend on the agreed work.
